How to use your jar

It is simpler than your old routine. Three steps, then get on with your day.

1

Warm a little

Scoop a small amount, less than you think, and warm it between clean fingertips until it turns to oil.

2

Massage in

Press it into clean, dry skin: face, hands, elbows, anywhere tight or dry. Massage until it disappears.

3

A little goes far

One jar lasts. Use it morning, night, or after a shower when skin is still a touch damp.

Our Story

Two people, one jar, made daily.

The Daily Jar started the way most good things do: trying to make something better for ourselves. We wanted a moisturizer with an ingredient list we could actually read, so we learned to render grass-fed tallow and whip it by hand.

Friends asked for a jar, then their friends did, and somewhere past 400 jars it became the thing we do. Every batch is still small, still poured by the two of us, still scented with real botanicals instead of a label that just says "fragrance."
